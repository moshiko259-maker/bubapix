From idea to film

Five stages. The frames show the film gaining colour as it takes shape.

  1. 1 It Starts with an Idea

    It can start with your idea, a product or business need, or an idea we develop together.

  2. 2 We Shape the Story

    We write the script and build the story scene by scene, until the message is clear.

  3. 3 We Build the World

    We design the characters, places and visual style, and approve them with you before production.

  4. 4 We Create the Film

    We generate, direct and refine every shot until it meets a cinematic standard.

  5. 5 Ready for the Screen

    We edit, add sound and music, grade the colour, and deliver in every format you need.

About BubaPix

BubaPix is an independent studio where filmmaking craft meets generative tools. We treat AI as a camera, not a shortcut: every film still needs a story worth telling, a deliberate look, and someone who cares about each frame.

We make films for brands, musicians and independent storytellers, from a single scene to a complete film.
