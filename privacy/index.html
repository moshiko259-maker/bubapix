Privacy

  • What we collect: the display name you choose, your message, and your email address if you give it. We ask for nothing else.
  • Why: to review and answer your message. Comments and questions, and ideas you allow us to share, are published with your display name only after review. Private consultations are never published.
  • Your email address is never published. We use it only to answer you.
  • Ratings are anonymous. To allow one rating per project and to stop spam, we keep a one-way coded form of your internet address, never the address itself.
  • Where: the data is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
  • To ask us to remove something you sent, write to us through the private consultation form.
